# Third-party notices

The optional nationwide US postal registry is derived from GeoNames postal data:
https://download.geonames.org/export/zip/

Data is provided under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The installer filters to US entries in the 50 states and Washington, DC and converts the source to a ZIP-to-state JSON mapping. Source URL, retrieval time, source SHA-256 and license are recorded in the generated registry. GeoNames does not endorse this application; this is not USPS street-address certification. The small bundled development fixture is explicitly labelled and is not nationwide data.

Python packages are installed separately under their respective package licenses. No third-party font files are included in this source distribution. Review and retain upstream notices when building and distributing deployment images.
