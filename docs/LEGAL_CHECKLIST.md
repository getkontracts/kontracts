# Legal and policy launch checklist

This is an implementation checklist, not legal advice or a statement of compliance. Have a qualified adviser review the service in the operator's actual jurisdiction and intended US customer markets.

Publish real operator identity/address and a single working support mailbox. Explain the $49 USD monthly subscription, applicable tax, 14-day no-card trial and explicit activation, cancellation/renewal, service limits, provider fees and how old subscriptions are handled. Clearly separate Kontracts software charges from the detailer's service contract, deposit, cancellation/refund terms and Square account.

Review the electronic-record consent, hardware/software/access disclosures, demonstrated PDF access, email verification, paper-copy/withdrawal process, record availability, attribution and retention. A typed signature plus email access is not identity certification or certificate-based PDF signing. No software-only statement guarantees enforceability in every transaction. The ESIGN statute is one relevant primary source, not the whole applicable legal framework: https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?req=granuleid:USC-prelim-title15-section7001&num=0&edition=prelim

Document personal-data categories (customer/job/address/photos/email/audit/signature records), purposes, subprocessors, access controls, data locations/transfers, retention, correction/deletion requests, incident handling and exports. Publish the actual retention policy, not an invented legal duration. Review US ZIP coverage exclusions and service-tax/invoice requirements. The invoice UI is not tax or accounting advice.

Create UTF-8 `legal/terms.txt` and `legal/privacy.txt`, 300-100000 characters each, after review. Set their paths plus LEGAL_NAME, LEGAL_ADDRESS and LEGAL_REVIEW_CONFIRMED=true. These are startup gates, not automated verification of the quality of legal review. No pre-approved policy text is supplied and setting the flag does not create compliance.
