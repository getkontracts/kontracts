# Single-plan pricing research

Research date: 17 September 2026. Recommendation implemented: **$49 USD per business per month, plus applicable tax**. No annual plan, upgrades or add-on tiers. One operator and calendar per business. All normal branding is included rather than sold as an upgrade.

## Market context

Urable publicly lists US Express at $70/month and Pro at $110/month. Its plan table puts online booking in Pro; it also supports broader staffing/integrations than this product. OrbisX advertises a $100/month promotional offer for a broader product. These are positioning references, not like-for-like feature equivalence and not a claim that competitors charge booking commissions.

At $49, this narrower solo-detailer product is $21/month below Urable Express. That is a reasonable initial price to validate with actual customers, not a guarantee of demand or profitability. Retain one clear price rather than an artificial three-tier grid.

Paddle's advertised standard fee is 5% + $0.50 per checkout transaction. Illustratively, a $49 transaction leaves $46.05 after that fee, before hosting, mailbox/email, storage/backups, support, taxes, refunds and other costs. Ten such customers would yield $460.50 before those costs. These are arithmetic scenarios, not forecasts; actual account terms, tax treatment and fees must be confirmed with the provider. Re-evaluate after measuring support time, invoice volume, storage and churn.

## What is sold

Approval-first booking page and quotes; business branding including logo/cover/favicon/button colors; service pricing; one calendar; customer records; transactional email; detailer/customer e-signatures and saved PDF invoices; Square or in-person collection; exports; 30 MiB optimized photo workspace. The product does not promise unlimited storage, perfect delivery, identity certification or accounting compliance.

Excluded: SMS, multi-employee dispatch, routing, native mobile apps, accounting or tax advice, managed custom domains, a hosted customer-support inbox, and unlimited photo storage. A 14-day no-card software trial requires explicit subscription activation afterward.

## Money flows

Customer -> detailer's own Square account, subject to Square fees/settlement, OR customer -> detailer in person. Kontracts does not collect job money or charge a booking commission. Detailer -> Paddle -> Kontracts covers the software subscription only. Do not add application fees or a destination/platform-account split to Square service-payment requests.

The software does not waive Square or Paddle processing fees. Service disputes/refunds are separate from subscription cancellation. Existing paid subscriptions retain their contracted provider price until explicitly migrated with the customer; the UI does not sell the old tiers.
