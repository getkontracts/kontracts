# One public subscription

Kontracts offers one subscription: **$49 USD per business per month, plus applicable tax**. One operator, one calendar and the included standard features. There is no plan comparison grid, annual offer or paid feature tier in this release. A new account can use the existing 14 day no card app trial, which does not itself create a paid subscription.

Included: approval based booking and quotes; city and ZIP coverage; service and vehicle pricing/duration; standard business branding; customer records, private notes and in-app conversations; queued email notifications; private reviews; optimized photos within the existing workspace quota; signed PDF invoices; direct payment records or Square payments; and authenticated exports.

Not included: SMS, multiple staff calendars, routing, native mobile applications, accounting or tax advice, new managed custom domains, a public review network or unlimited storage. Private request conversations do not turn notification emails into a hosted inbound email inbox.

Square processes the customer's job payment for the detailer. Paddle handles the detailer's Kontracts subscription. The new Get Square link is only a disclosed referral link; clicking it does not connect an account or create a payment.

Existing contracted provider prices are retained until an explicit authorized migration. Legacy price identifiers are accepted for existing subscriptions, not offered as extra subscription types. Provider fees are separate and depend on the account's actual terms. No profitability forecast or fee waiver is promised.
